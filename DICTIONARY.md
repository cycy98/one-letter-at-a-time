# Dictionary rules

## Normalization

- Remove diacritics and lowercase all words, including proper nouns.
- Use modern Latin spellings. Other scripts and Old English letters are not
  permitted unless a standard modern spelling exists.
- Expand ligatures; accepted variants may omit a ligature's first letter.
- Remove punctuation other than hyphens and apostrophes, replace it with a
  hyphen, or spell out its name.
- Collapse repeated punctuation and remove leading hyphens.

## Eligibility

- A listing in a non-user-generated dictionary is sufficient evidence.
- User-generated sources must be at least one year old and verifiable. Comments,
  social-media posts, and blogs are not sources.
- LLMs, other text generators, are not sources.
- Fictional words from sufficiently popular games are allowed, except words from
  word games.
- Inflections and ordinary derivations require less supporting evidence.
- An abbreviation and its source phrase may both be included; encode the phrase
  with hyphens.

Numeric terms follow these additional rules:

- A number name is allowed only when it is one word or represents 0 through 100.
- Terms whose number can be replaced by any integer are excluded, except
  one-word number names and qualifying mathematical terms.
- A term with at least one qualifying non-arbitrary use may be written out and
  hyphenated, such as `one-hundred-percent` or `three-hundred-sixty-degree`.

## Scientific terms

- Chemistry terms must describe real or discovered substances or elements.
- Shape names above ten dimensions are excluded. Other shape names must be
  common or belong to a finite category.
- `-illion`, `-illard`, `-yllion`, and similar systems are limited to numeral
  prefixes below one thousand.

Entries are limited to 100 characters.
